# Personas and scenarios

## Primary user
Owns several ECO-WORTHY LiFePO4 batteries and uses them for backup power and camping.
Technically comfortable and wants detailed data and control.

## Scenario A: Power outage at home
- No grid power. Internet and cellular may be down.
- Batteries power essentials for hours or days.
- Needs: remaining runtime at a glance, a low-SOC warning before loads drop,
  and the ability to shut off discharge on a battery remotely.
- Constraint: the phone's own battery is limited, so the app must be efficient.

## Scenario B: Camping, off grid
- No cellular coverage. Solar charging during the day.
- Needs: charge progress, an alert when a battery is full or overheating,
  and a check that solar is actually charging.
- Batteries may be in a vehicle, a tent, or near the campsite, so Bluetooth range matters.

## Scenario C: Storage and maintenance
- Batteries in storage between uses.
- Needs: a periodic check on SOC and cell balance, and a reminder to top them up.
